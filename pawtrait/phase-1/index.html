A professional portrait without asking them to sit for one.

Professional pet portraiture is having a moment, and it's easy to see why — our pets deserve the same care and attention we give every meaningful relationship. But a traditional studio session isn't right for every dog. Some won't sit still. Some become overwhelmed in unfamiliar spaces. Others are older, anxious, or simply most themselves at home.

PAWTRAIT begins with a photograph you already love. Our AI-assisted process helps transform that image into a polished, studio-style portrait without asking your pet to pose, travel or perform. Human review protects the details that matter most: their eyes, expression, coloring and familiar presence.

Frequently asked questions
What kind of photograph should I upload?
Choose a clear, well-lit photo showing your pet's face, eyes and natural expression. A headshot taken near eye level usually produces the strongest result.
Does my pet need to attend a photo session?
No. PAWTRAIT begins with a photograph you already have, so your pet stays comfortable at home — especially helpful for pets who are anxious, reactive, older or unlikely to sit for a studio session.
Will I see my personalized design before purchasing?
Yes. You'll see your pet's actual portrait on your actual pillow and approve it yourself before you pay — nothing is a surprise.
How is AI used?
AI assists in transforming your submitted photograph into the PAWTRAIT visual style. Human review helps protect your pet's recognizable features. AI doesn't choose your phrase for you.
Can I choose my pet's phrase?
Yes. Tell us about your pet's personality and choose a tone. We'll show you a curated collection of matching phrases — you can also write your own.
Are the linen texture and lettering physically woven or embroidered?
No — the linen-inspired texture and embroidered-style lettering are printed as part of the design, creating the look and character of textile details without physical stitching.
Can I order the cover without an insert?
Yes. Both sizes are available as a cover only or as a complete pillow with an insert.
What can I change after I order?
You have 24 hours to request a quick fix — correcting a name or date, or choosing a different phrase from your original options. Because your design was already approved before purchase, this window is for small corrections, not a full redesign.
What if I'm not satisfied when it arrives?
Contact us within 30 days of delivery. Return the complete product you purchased and we'll refund the product price and applicable sales tax. Original shipping isn't refundable, and return shipping is your responsibility.
Can I create a PAWTRAIT for a pet who has passed away?
Yes. We approach memorial PAWTRAITs with particular care, because the relationship doesn't end when a lifetime does.
